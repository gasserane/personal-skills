# Stage 1 — TOPIC: the series ledger and the next-topic proposal

## The ledger

`field-notes-ledger.md` in the LinkedIn content folder is the single source of truth for the series: what shipped, what is drafted, what is parked, how the audience reacted, and what the next topics could be. Every stage of this skill reads it first and updates it last.

### Schema

```markdown
# AI Field Notes — series ledger

## Series rules
- Hashtags (every article and post): #AIFieldNotes #MEL #Evaluation #ResponsibleAI #AIforNGOs
- Signature line (visuals only, mono): The AI drafts, the system verifies, I decide.
- Publishing mechanics: article first, then the short post, then immediately the
  first comment with the article link. Toolkits ship as PDF document posts.
- Calendar (agreed 2026-07-27): Tuesday 09:00-10:30 RST toolkit document post;
  Wednesday or Thursday 08:30-10:00 RST article + short post. Post only when Ane
  can engage the first 60-90 minutes.

## Field notes

### #01 — <title>
- Status: published | approved | drafted | parked | idea
- Dates: drafted YYYY-MM-DD; published YYYY-MM-DD
- Artifacts: article <path>; post <path>; toolkit <path>; visuals <paths>
- First comment: <text or path>
- Reactions and learning: <what Ane reports after posting>
- Seeds: <follow-up topics this note opened>
```

One `###` block per note, in series order. Ideas without a number yet go under a final `### Parked and ideas` block.

## First run — bootstrap the ledger

If the ledger file does not exist:

1. List the content folder and group files by field-note number and date prefix.
2. Known catalogue at skill creation (2026-07-27), to speed the pass: #01 = 33-agent AI system (article + v3 ULTRA-SHORT post + Three-Agent Starter Kit / Start-with-three toolkit companion); #02 = why AI systems for MEL make sense (article DRAFT + post "what evaluators teach AI" DRAFT); #03 = risks of AI in MEL and MEL in AI (article DRAFT + post DRAFT). Header/cover PNGs exist for all three. Re-verify against the folder — files move and ship between sessions.
3. Draft the ledger, then **confirm statuses with Ane in one batched question** (published vs drafted vs parked, and actual publish dates). Filenames say "DRAFT" but only Ane knows what went live. Never guess a status.
4. Write the file only after she confirms.

## Proposing the next topic

1. Read the ledger: seeds from published notes, parked ideas, reactions Ane reported.
2. Cross-check what Ane is currently working on (conversation, memory, recent session notes) for a timely angle.
3. Propose 2-3 candidate topics. For each: one-line pitch, why now (ledger seed, audience reaction, or current work), and what the companion toolkit would be. Lead with your recommended one and the load-bearing reason, grill-mel style.
4. Ane picks or redirects. Record the choice in the ledger (status: idea → drafted pipeline begins) and move to Stage 2.

A field note needs all three legs before Stage 3 ends: an article-sized argument, a post-sized version, and one attachable artifact the article can promise. If a candidate topic has no plausible toolkit, say so in the pitch — Ane may still choose it and drop the toolkit leg deliberately.
