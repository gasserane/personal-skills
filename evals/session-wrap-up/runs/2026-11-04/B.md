### Questions to settle before the survey is final

- **Clients under 18.** Volunteers will survey young clients, and some clients may be under 18. The consent session and the survey introduction need to say what applies to minors, for example whether a parent must agree and how answers stay confidential. ⚠️ Data gap: I don't know your clinic's rule on consent for minors. It changes what the 10:15 session teaches, so please check the rule or tell me what it is.
- **Meaning of "safe" in Question 4.** Young clients may read "safe" as physical safety, privacy, or not being judged. A pilot with two or three clients would show which reading they use. You could also add one short line on what "safe" means here. Your role-play case is about privacy, which suggests privacy is the concern behind the question.
- **Phone version.** Reading the survey aloud over the phone can make it harder to give an honest answer on Questions 2 and 4. Consider letting clients answer by text or by number only.
- **Romanian translation.** If you want it, I can do a back-translation check after the translator finishes. This means translating the Romanian back into English to confirm the 1 to 5 labels and the word "safe" kept their meaning.
