## Questions to settle before the survey is final

- **Question wording against the scale.** Questions 2 and 4 read as yes or no questions, but they use a 1 to 5 scale. For example, "How safe did you feel during your visit?" fits the scale better. If last year's survey used the same wording, keep it so you can compare. Check this when you share last year's survey.
- **Clients under 18.** Your example client was 16, so some survey respondents will be minors. ⚠️ Data gap: we have not discussed your organisation's consent rule for clients under 18. It decides who may answer the survey and what the 10:15 consent session must teach. Please confirm your rule or tell us who in your organisation sets it.
- **What volunteers do if a client says they did not feel safe.** The new question 4 may lead a client to describe abuse or another serious problem. Volunteers need a clear step for passing this on (who to tell, and how), and the consent session or the role play should practise it. We have not designed this step yet.
