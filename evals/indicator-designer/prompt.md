I need a small indicator set for a project proposal, and I have until tomorrow morning.

A Member Association (MA) plans a two-year project (illustrative, for this exercise) to improve young people's access to contraception in three peri-urban districts. About 12,000 young people aged 15-24 live in the catchment area. The project will:

- open one evening and Saturday youth-friendly session per week in each of the three existing clinics;
- train 30 community health workers as peer referrers.

Outcome statement the donor wants measured: "Young people aged 15-24 in the three districts use the contraceptive method they choose."

What we can and cannot collect: the MA has clinic registers (paper, summarised monthly) and can run one household survey at baseline and one at endline. It has no budget for a third survey. Reporting to the donor is every six months. We want to keep one indicator we already use: "number of young people receiving contraceptive services."

The indicators serve donor accountability and also help the MA's programme team adjust the project during delivery.

Give me 4 to 6 indicators for the results framework: a mix of output and outcome level, with definitions, disaggregation, data source and a note on how reliable each one is. Write it for programme staff who are not MEL specialists. Keep it practical.
